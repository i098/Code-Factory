How this harbor is built

  • Renderer. Every frame casts one ray per character cell from your eye. Buildings, the dock and the hull are convex solids made of planes. Masts, barrels, tree trunks and the lighthouse are true cones with smooth normals. Rocks and tree canopies are ellipsoids. The sea is a height field of travelling waves, ray marched per cell; the ship and the boats ride it.
  • Light. It is night: lamps, lit windows, the lantern and the beacon light what is near them, fall off with distance and cast shadows from the solids around them; the lighthouse beam sweeps; the moon gives a dim fill. The sea shows Fresnel reflection, moon and lamp highlights and foam. Fog and contact shadows add depth.
  • Characters. Brightness usually picks a glyph from the density ramp .`',:;~-=+*#%@▒▓█ and one of eight colour levels per material. Grass uses blade glyphs; pale paving marks and foam keep their texture glyphs with a minimum brightness that decreases in fog. Fountain water uses animated texture glyphs instead of the density ramp. An edge glyph (| - / \ _) is drawn wherever one surface meets a farther one.
  • Frame loop. requestAnimationFrame moves you, the sea and the ship and redraws the grid on a canvas. Devices with (pointer: coarse) draw one glyph per fillText call to limit WebKit's text cache. Their canvas uses at most 2 device pixels per CSS pixel. Other devices draw one run of one colour per call. The font is ready before the first grid measurement. For a fixed viewport, the glyph size stays fixed; slow frames can disable shadows without changing the view. Resizes wait until the viewport settles, then update the canvas immediately before the next draw. With reduced motion the scene stands still and redraws only when you move.
  • Controls. WASD or arrows walk and turn, R and F look up and down, the mouse looks around, Enter opens what you point at. On a phone, the bottom-left pad walks; drag on the right to look around. The pad, labels, intro card, and map controls respect safe-area insets. In landscape, the intro card leaves the map visible. Feature labels float beside their objects, with a leader line in text, and hide when their objects are behind you. On a phone, feature labels stay above the pad; scroll tall labels to reach their links.
  • Mini map. M picks a point on the text map in the corner, M again fills the screen with it; arrows choose, Enter jumps there. On a phone, tap a point.
  • Content. harbor/build.py builds the points of interest with the README's Features list and More docs row, and the changelog.
  • Deploy. harbor/ is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no dependencies. A GitHub Actions workflow builds it and deploys it to Cloudflare Workers static assets on every push to main and on each release; nothing in harbor/ ever reaches a Crewship host or image.
