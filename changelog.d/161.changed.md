The crewship.si scene culls solids per screen tile and redraws only changed cells, so 10 s of play at 1440x900 in Chromium on an Apple M5 goes from 46-54 to a steady 60 fps with shadows kept, iOS Simulator Safari (iPhone 15 Pro) goes from 59-60 to a steady 60 fps, and a slower test host goes from 6.6-10.4 to 17-27 fps, with about ten times fewer object tests per frame.
